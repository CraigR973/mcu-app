# Movie-collection mark concepts — round 1

Six square concept studies for the compact mark in the Mark's Cinematic Universe identity. These are visual direction tests, not production logo masters.

## Shared brief

- Communicate a curated collection of physically owned films rather than streaming.
- Integrate an `M` into the object or its negative space.
- Use near-black `#0A0A0B`, champagne gold `#D4AF7F`, and restrained burgundy `#B8475E`.
- Aim for a centered, geometric mark that can ultimately be redrawn as a flat vector.
- Avoid text, the Marvel name, clapperboards, popcorn, and play-button shorthand.

## Concepts

| No. | Direction | Core idea | First-round observation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Case + Disc | An open physical case forms the `M`, with a partial disc on the right | Strongest combination of ownership, cinema, and an ownable `M`; survives reduction well |
| 2 | Cinematic Shelf | Five case spines create an `M` skyline on a shelf | Clearest collection signal and excellent small-size silhouette; cinema is less explicit |
| 3 | Film Vault | Disc/reel held in an archival drawer | Distinctive and premium, but the drawer can read as storage hardware before movie archive |
| 4 | Poster Stack | Three cases/posters with a disc behind | Most cinematic and expressive at display size; too detailed for a primary micro mark |
| 5 | Collector's Seal | `M`, disc hub, and film perforations in a circular badge | Strong badge/app-icon behavior; collection meaning is implicit and the silhouette is less distinctive |
| 6 | Catalogue Frame | Film/contact-sheet frame becomes a collection grid and `M` | Organised, scalable, and UI-adjacent; risks reading as a generic gallery/grid |

## Small-size test

Each source was reduced directly to 32 px with Lanczos resampling, then enlarged with nearest-neighbour scaling for inspection in `concept-comparison-32px.png`. This exposes structural legibility rather than judging the polish of the generated previews.

## Production note

The generated studies contain tonal modelling and soft edge glow despite the flat-art brief. Whichever direction is selected should be redrawn as clean vector geometry with optical corrections, uniform line weights, true flat colour, and dedicated 16/32 px variants.
