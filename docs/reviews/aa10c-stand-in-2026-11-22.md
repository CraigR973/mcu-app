## What was compared

- **Sales:** every sold listing recorded by AA10a (status `sold`, a positive sold count, ended in
  the last 90 days), its last price with postage, each listing once (#291). 1,824 sales over 913
  discs (film and format), 22 Oct – 22 Nov. By week of ending: 181, 459, 517, 416, and 251 for the
  week of 16 Nov, which was still filling.
- **The stand-in:** the stored value where it is AA10b's lower quartile of used Buy It Now asks,
  postage in (stored 23 Oct – 15 Nov).
- **Today's used median:** the stored median of used asks, postage left out: #289's "today" rule,
  the figure before AA10b.
- **Error on a disc:** (figure − sales median) / sales median. Positive reads high.
- **Which is closer:** the smaller median absolute error (the typical miss on one disc), over
  discs with 3 or more sales: the owner's choice, 22 Nov. A tie keeps the lower quartile.
- **Check:** the sales medians here equal the stored `sales_price` and count on all 209
  sales-backed rows.
